MyFinance Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 14, 2026

MyFinance is developed by CodeZero Interactive. This policy explains how the Android application handles information.

Information stored on your device

MyFinance stores financial records that you enter, including accounts, balances, expenses, income, categories, budgets, fixed payments, settings, and locally generated backups. These records are stored locally on your device using SQLite. MyFinance does not provide a cloud account, server synchronization, or remote financial database.

Receipts and OCR

If you choose a receipt or transfer image, the image is accessed only to perform the feature you requested. Text recognition is performed on-device through the installed OCR component. The application does not upload receipt images or recognized receipt text to a MyFinance server.

Currency conversion

When you explicitly request a transfer between accounts with different currencies, MyFinance may request a public exchange rate from the Frankfurter exchange-rate service. The request contains only the selected currency codes. Financial records, account names, receipt images, and transaction notes are not sent for currency conversion. Exchange rates may be cached locally for up to 24 hours so that a previously used rate can be available without another request.

Permissions

MyFinance may request access to photos or media only when you choose to select a receipt image. You can deny this permission; manual financial entry remains available.

Security

The optional app PIN is stored using the device secure storage service. The PIN helps prevent access through the app interface. You are responsible for keeping your device protected. Deleting the app, clearing its application data, or restoring an incompatible backup can remove locally stored records.

Data sharing and sale

MyFinance does not sell, rent, share, or use your financial records for advertising. The app does not include advertising SDKs or analytics SDKs that collect your financial records.

Backups and exports

Backups and CSV exports are created only when you request them. You decide where to save, share, or delete those files. MyFinance cannot control how other applications or services handle a file after you share it.

Children

MyFinance is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when app features or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published at this address and the updated date will be revised.
